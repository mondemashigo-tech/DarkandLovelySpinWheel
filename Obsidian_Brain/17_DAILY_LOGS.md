# 📅 Daily Logs — Index & Template

> The intake valve of the whole brain ([[06_MEMORY_SYSTEM]]). Linked from [[00_README]]

## The template

Daily notes are named `YYYY-MM-DD.md` (Obsidian daily-notes plugin) and use:

```
---
tags: [daily]
---
# {YYYY-MM-DD}

## Priorities (max 3)
- [ ]
- [ ]
- [ ]

## Capture
<!-- anything: ideas, links, quotes, screenshots — zero formatting required -->

## Shutdown (3 lines)
- Done:
- Learned:
- Next:
```

## Rules

- The **shutdown 3-liner is non-negotiable** ([[02_MONDE]]) — 60 seconds, every day.
- Capture is append-only during the day; processing happens at shutdown or weekly review ([[04_OPERATING_SYSTEM]]).
- WhatsApp self-chat and voice notes get emptied into today's Capture section.
- Anything still valuable after the weekly review gets promoted per [[06_MEMORY_SYSTEM]]; the rest stays here as history.
