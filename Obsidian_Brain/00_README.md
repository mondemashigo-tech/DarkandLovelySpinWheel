## How this vault works

- **Daily capture** flows in through [[17_DAILY_LOGS]] and gets distilled upward into the permanent notes.

## Rules of the vault

1. **Capture first, organize later.** Anything worth keeping goes into today's daily log immediately.
2. **One idea per note** once a topic outgrows its section.
3. **Link or it didn't happen.** An unlinked note is a lost note.
4. **Review weekly.** The [[04_OPERATING_SYSTEM]] weekly review keeps this alive.
