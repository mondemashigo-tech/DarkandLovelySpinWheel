## Change rules

1. **The numbered backbone (00–19) is stable.** Notes evolve in place; numbers are never reused or reordered.
2. **Structural changes are logged here** the day they happen — structure that lives only in chat gets lost (lesson: [[16_LEARNING_LOG]], 2026-07-15).
3. **Friction is the signal.** Couldn't find something twice? Skipped a ritual twice? That's a structural bug — log it below and fix the system, not the discipline.
4. **Monthly changelog entry** even if the entry is "no changes" ([[04_OPERATING_SYSTEM]]).
