> Honest retros only ([[01_TAO]]). Linked from [[00_README]] · Fed by [[15_EXPERIMENTS]], [[11_DECISION_FRAMEWORKS]], [[17_DAILY_LOGS]]

## How to log

One entry per lesson, newest first. Three lines max:

```
### YYYY-MM-DD — {one-line lesson}
- What happened: {facts, no spin}
- What changes: {the rule/SOP/note updated because of this}
```

A lesson that doesn't change a note or a rule isn't finished — every entry must end with a link to what it changed.
