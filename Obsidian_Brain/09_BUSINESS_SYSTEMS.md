## Pipeline stages

```
LEAD → QUALIFIED → PROPOSAL → WON → DELIVERED → RETAINED
```

- Every lead gets a row in the pipeline tracker within 24h.
- Proposals use the template in [[14_SOPS]]; pricing follows the rules below.
- Weekly review ([[04_OPERATING_SYSTEM]]) updates every open row.

## Pricing rules

1. Price the outcome, not the hours.
2. 50% upfront on all client work — no exceptions.
3. Scope changes are new line items, quoted before work continues.
4. Decisions on discounts go through [[11_DECISION_FRAMEWORKS]] — never decided in the moment.

## Money hygiene

- Separate accounts: operating / tax / profit.
- Monthly numbers reviewed at the monthly ritual; honest numbers only ([[01_TAO]]).
- Every experiment with paid ads or tooling gets a budget cap in [[15_EXPERIMENTS]] before it starts.
