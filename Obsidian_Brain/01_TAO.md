# ☯️ The TAO — Guiding Philosophy

> The "why" underneath everything. When in doubt, come back here.
> Linked from: [[00_README]] · Feeds: [[11_DECISION_FRAMEWORKS]], [[02_MONDE]]

## Core principles

1. **Zero point thinking.** Start from nothing, assume nothing. Every project begins from first principles, not from how it was done before.
2. **Ship small, ship often.** A finished small thing beats a perfect unfinished thing. Momentum is the strategy.
3. **Systems over willpower.** If it must happen more than twice, it becomes a system — see [[10_AUTOMATION]] and [[14_SOPS]].
4. **Leverage compounds.** Code, content, and capital all compound. Spend time on things that keep working after the work stops.
5. **The brain remembers so I don't have to.** Capture everything into [[06_MEMORY_SYSTEM]]; keep the mind free for creating.
6. **Quality is the brand.** Nothing leaves the studio below the bar in [[08_CREATIVE_STANDARDS]].
7. **Truth over comfort.** Honest numbers, honest retros — [[16_LEARNING_LOG]] only works if it's honest.

## Operating mantras

- *Direction over speed — but once direction is set, speed.*
- *Done is a decision.*
- *If it's not written down, it's a rumour.*
- *Every experiment is a win: either it works or it teaches — [[15_EXPERIMENTS]].*

## What we say no to

- Work that doesn't compound and doesn't pay.
- Tools that add steps instead of removing them.
- Commitments made without checking [[04_OPERATING_SYSTEM]] capacity.

## Review

Revisit this note at every monthly review. If a principle hasn't influenced a real decision in 90 days, question it in [[19_EVOLUTION]].
