# 🎨 Creative Standards — The Quality Bar

> Nothing ships below this line. Linked from [[00_README]] · Enforced at Gate 2/3 of [[07_PROJECT_FRAMEWORK]]

## Brand voice (ZPK)

- **Confident, not loud.** We show the work; we don't hype it.
- **Clear over clever.** If a first-time user hesitates, we rewrite.
- **African-rooted, globally fluent.** Local references worn naturally, never as costume.

## Visual principles

1. Dark, rich backgrounds; one bold accent per piece.
2. Motion with purpose — every animation communicates state, never decoration alone (the spin wheel is the reference implementation).
3. Typography does the heavy lifting; imagery supports.
4. Mobile-first always — most of our audience meets us on a phone.

## Shipping checklist (any public asset)

- [ ] Works on a mid-range Android phone
- [ ] Loads in under 3 seconds on mobile data
- [ ] Copy read aloud once — no stumbles
- [ ] Brand accent + voice consistent with this note
- [ ] Someone other than the maker has used it cold

## Client work addendum

- Client brand rules override ZPK visuals but never override the **quality checklist**.
