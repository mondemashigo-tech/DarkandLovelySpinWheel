# 🧭 Decision Frameworks — Choosing Fast and Well

> Used at every project gate and money decision. Linked from [[00_README]] · Rooted in [[01_TAO]]

## The default filter (any opportunity)

Ask in order — a "no" at any step ends it:

1. **Does it compound?** (skill, asset, audience, or cash that keeps working)
2. **Can v1 ship in ≤ 2 weeks?** ([[07_PROJECT_FRAMEWORK]] rule)
3. **Does it fit the current focus** in [[02_MONDE]]? If not, park it — don't kill it.
4. **What does it cost if it fails?** Cap the downside before starting ([[15_EXPERIMENTS]] budget rule).

## Reversible vs irreversible

- **Reversible** (most things): decide fast, decide cheap, adjust later.
- **Irreversible** (contracts, brand moves, public commitments): sleep on it, write the downside case, decide next day.

## Money decisions

- Under R1 000: just decide, log it.
- R1 000+ or a day+ of work: write 3 lines — *cost, expected return, kill condition* — before committing ([[02_MONDE]] rule).

## When stuck between two options

- **10/10/10:** how will this feel in 10 days / 10 months / 10 years?
- **Regret test:** at zero point, starting fresh — which would I choose?
- **Energy test:** which option would I actually work on at 21:00 on a Tuesday?

## After the decision

Outcome + reasoning gets 3 lines in [[16_LEARNING_LOG]]. Calibration only improves if predictions are written down.
